Decision Chart · Nifty 50 DEMO · simulated data

Each line is one time frame's decision (up = bullish / BUY CE, down = bearish / BUY PE, flat = wait). Candles are 1-minute Nifty prices.
How to read it: the thin strip under the candles is the agreement ribbon — bright green when most time frames are bullish together, bright red when most are bearish, dark when they disagree. ▲ / ▼ markers appear when the 1m line crosses the 5m line while the 15m line already points the same way (a sample rule only — the real rule will be designed from recorded data). Drag to pan, mouse-wheel or ＋/－ to zoom, hover (or touch) for all scores at that minute. The 1D / 1W / 1M decisions barely move within a day, so they are shown as chips above the chart instead of lines.
This page uses made-up prices and scores so you can judge the look and feel. It is not connected to the live app and is not trading advice.
